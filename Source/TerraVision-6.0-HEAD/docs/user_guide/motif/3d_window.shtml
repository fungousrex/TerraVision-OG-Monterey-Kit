The 3-D Viewer "Window" Menu

Show Menu Bar

This option can be used to toggle the display of the menu bar at the top of the window. You can also perform this operation by pressing 'M' on the keyboard. The keyboard shortcut is important to know because once you have hidden the menu bar, you cannot access the menu option to bring the menu bar back!

Show Web Icons

This option can be used to toggle the display of the web icons panel at the bottom of the window. You can also perform this operation by pressing 'W' on the keyboard.

Show Status Bar

This option can be used to toggle the display of the status bar at the bottom of the window. This gives you various pieces of information including your current navigation mode and view mode. You can also perform this operation by pressing 'A' on the keyboard.

Show Fullscreen

This option toggles the 3-D Viewer fullscreen capability. When selected, the viewer will be expanded to fill the entire screen. Then when you deselect this option, the window will return to its previous size and position. You can also perform this operation by pressing 'F' on the keyboard.
